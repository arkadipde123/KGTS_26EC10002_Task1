My favourite board game

Ludo

Ludo is one of the few board games that can't really be cracked by clever tricks or deep strategy — it's almost purely random. That's exactly why I love it. There's no meta to optimise, no edge to chase, just the dice and whatever happens next. It's a game of pure fun, not calculation — which makes for a nice change of pace from everything this society is about.
